Scout Passport — interactive prototype

Clickable. Log in, switch tabs, answer the challenge, flip the language, and toggle into the leader view. No backend — state resets when you reload.

Ρόλος / Role

Leader = Δέσποινα, responsible for 🐺 Λύκοι only.
Troop lead = Κυριάκος, sees everything and assigns who manages what.

Try this

  • Log in with any 8-digit passcode (e.g. 1234-5678)
  • Tap ΕΛ / EN in the header — the whole UI flips
  • Tap a badge on the passport for its detail sheet
  • Answer the challenge — one attempt, instant grading, points update everywhere
  • Filter the calendar by troop / section / patrol
  • Open Χρήσιμα / Info → Στολές for the uniform page
  • Settings is the ⚙ in the passport header — tap Preview push there

As a leader

Leaders don't get the scout app — a brief Προφίλ tab, then only management screens for their assigned sector.

  • Leader (Δέσποινα): sees only 🐺 Λύκοι everywhere — scouts, review, challenges, announcements
  • Προκλήσεις → she can author, locked to her sector
  • Troop lead (Κυριάκος): all sectors, and Άλλα → Ρόλοι & δικαιώματα to assign who manages what
  • Πτυχία now lives under Άλλα
